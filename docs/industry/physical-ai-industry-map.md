## How Elaris analyzes each actor

For each actor, document only:

1. **Role** — what they do.
2. **Decision** — what they must decide.
3. **Inputs** — what information they need.
4. **Outputs** — what they produce.
5. **Pain** — where work is fragmented, duplicated or delayed.
6. **Elaris opportunity** — what Elaris could make easier.
