# A12 — Legal / Compliance / Risk Governance

**Lifecycle:** Cross-cutting governance  
**Evidence status:** `ARCHETYPE_HYPOTHESIS` unless a pilot explicitly proves otherwise.

## Role
Interprets contractual, legal, policy and data-governance obligations.

## Decisions
- obligations/disclosures
- data-use authorization
- incident/change escalation and retention

## Triggers
- contract/deployment
- data sharing/export
- incident
- request
- renewal

## Inputs
- contracts/policies
- deployment facts
- provenance
- approvals
- incident chronology

## Outputs
- legal conditions
- data-use restriction
- retention/disclosure instruction
- governance record

## Pain hypothesis
- technical facts hard to reconstruct
- ownership/export ambiguous
- approval history fragmented

## Authority boundary
Human legal/compliance authority; Elaris provides evidence, not legal advice.

**Primary:** None assigned yet.

## Artifacts to request
- contract/schedule
- DPA/data approval
- policy
- legal memo
- retention record

## Discovery
Start with: **“Mostrame la última vez que tomaron esta decisión con un sistema Physical AI real.”**

Reconstruct trigger, owner, source systems, artifacts, missing/repeated information, authority, output and what later change reopens the work.

## Validation gates
1. One real person/organization.
2. One recent real decision end-to-end.
3. At least one artifact set.
4. Named human authority and non-claims.
5. Mapping to shared Elaris truth without copying it.
6. Persistence/integration only after recurrence.

## Kill / merge
If there is no distinct recurring job, merge the use case into the adjacent Product System instead of creating another product.
