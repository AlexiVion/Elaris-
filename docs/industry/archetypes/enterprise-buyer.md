# A06 — Enterprise Buyer / Business Owner

**Lifecycle:** Enterprise adoption  
**Evidence status:** `ARCHETYPE_HYPOTHESIS` unless a pilot explicitly proves otherwise.

## Role
Sponsors/owns business outcome and accepts commercial/operational trade-offs.

## Decisions
- adopt/continue/expand
- conditions satisfied
- changes since accepted state

## Triggers
- pilot proposal
- go-live
- expansion
- material change
- renewal

## Inputs
- deployment scope
- readiness evidence
- commercial terms
- limits
- incident/service history

## Outputs
- go/no-go sponsorship
- acceptance conditions
- expansion/renewal decision

## Pain hypothesis
- technical truth hard to consume
- post-acceptance change invisible
- evidence rebuilt internally

## Authority boundary
Business acceptance authority only, not safety/certification authority.

## Elaris relationship
**Primary:** [Operational Readiness](../../product/operational-readiness/README.md)

**Relevant:** [Deployment Control / Deployment & Change Evidence](../../product/deployment-control/README.md), [Asset Monitoring](../../product/asset-monitoring/README.md), [Placement Workspace](../../product/placement-workspace/README.md), [Incident Reconstruction](../../product/incident-reconstruction/README.md)

## Artifacts to request
- business case
- pilot acceptance
- operating conditions
- renewal review

## Discovery
Start with: **“Mostrame la última vez que tomaron esta decisión con un sistema Physical AI real.”**

Reconstruct trigger, owner, source systems, artifacts, missing/repeated information, authority, output and what later change reopens the work.

## Validation gates
1. One real person/organization.
2. One recent real decision end-to-end.
3. At least one artifact set.
4. Named human authority and non-claims.
5. Mapping to shared Elaris truth without copying it.
6. Persistence/integration only after recurrence.

## Kill / merge
If there is no distinct recurring job, merge the use case into the adjacent Product System instead of creating another product.
